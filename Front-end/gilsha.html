THE CONCEPT

"A climate-responsive sanctuary blending Gilan's vernacular heritage with modern spatial poetry."

Designed around a centralized plan to optimize natural light and cross-ventilation, Gilsha Villa translates the local architectural language into a contemporary living experience. The 35-degree pitched roof elegantly manages rainfall, while a dynamic sliding wooden lattice on the western facade controls the region's intense winds and filters the warm evening sunlight.

Crafted with moisture-resistant microcement and handmade green clay tiles, the space seamlessly connects the indoors with the central garden. The dining area, featuring smart sliding glass, opens entirely to the landscape, creating a quiet retreat—a place to listen to the rain, find solitude, and experience the profound empathy of space.
