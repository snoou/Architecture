FOROUGH FARAHMAND

THE JOURNEY

I am Forough Farahmand; an architect, designer, and spatial researcher.

I began my professional career in 2014. My early years were spent collaborating with architectural firms and participating in design and construction projects. Later, alongside a colleague, I co-founded an independent architectural studio, gaining invaluable experience in teamwork, project management, and the development of architectural ideas. Subsequently, to realize my personal vision and worldview regarding architecture, I founded "Existence Architecture Office"—a place for reflection, exploration, and creation.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Interior Architecture and a Master's degree in Architecture, with over a decade of experience in designing and executing architectural projects. On my professional journey, what encourages me to keep going more than anything else is an endless passion for a deeper understanding of the relationship between human, place, and meaning.

PHILOSOPHY

"Architecture is the search for existence amidst light, matter, and silence."

I perceive architecture as the discovery of the hidden layers of existence. For me, every project is a journey into an unknown world; a world that resides in the silence of the earth, light, matter, and the unwritten memories of its inhabitants. Before designing, I listen: to the place, the time, the human, and to that which has not yet been spoken.

I have a profound devotion to designing details, as I believe the truth of architecture is often revealed in its most delicate moments. To me, details are not merely technical elements; they are the vocabulary of architectural poetry.

Alongside architecture, literature is my constant companion. I write poetry and literary texts, and upon the completion of each project, I compose a piece for the building—as if the structure is a living being that will breathe alongside humans for years. In these writings, the building is my addressee; I speak of its sufferings, its dreams, its memories, and the future that awaits it.

When writing, the boundary between reality and imagination fades for me. Words carry me beyond imagination, to places not yet built; worlds that exist only in dreams, intuition, and fantasy. Often, these mental journeys transform into visual narratives and cinematography scenarios; an attempt to express that part of architecture which cannot be explained through plans and images alone.

My interest in philosophy, mysticism, phenomenology, psychology, and intuitive thoughts also stems from a kind of quest; a search for a deeper meaning behind what is visible. My hobbies include playing the Hang drum, writing poetry and literary texts, and Vitray (glass painting) art. These are alternative moments to experience light, silence, rhythm, and presence.

For me, architecture is a quest that can ultimately forge a connection between the human soul and itself, time, and the universe.

OUR COMMITMENT

At Existence Architecture Office, we are committed to:

01 Creating a sanctuary based on the principles of sustainable and context-responsive architecture.
02 Technical precision with a tangible language in empathetic harmony with art.
03 Listening architecture: an inquiry into permanence and profound empathy with space.
04 In-depth consultations with audiences, because the space must reflect the inner identity of its inhabitants. And this continues until the internal narrative of the space is revealed.
05 Commitment to the human spirit.
06 Designing environments that act as a palliative for the noise of this era, and withstand the passage of time with grace and beauty.

We are constantly exploring how built forms can foster a deeper sense of belonging and tranquility in the modern world.
